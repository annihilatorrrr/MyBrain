# My Brain Privacy Policy

All data is stored locally on the device and is not sent to any server owned by me or any third party.

When using the optional Local Sync feature, app data is transferred directly between devices paired by the user. Sync data is encrypted during transfer and does not pass through a server owned by me or any third party. If the user configures a fallback address, sync traffic may pass through a network service chosen by the user.

When using the AI assistant feature, usage data is not collected by the app. However, please check the respective AI provider's privacy policy regarding their data handling practices.

There are no ads or trackers in the app and no data is collected.
